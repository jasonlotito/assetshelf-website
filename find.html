Filters that stay out of your way.

Open Filters as its own movable, resizable window. Change criteria and see the browser update without losing your place. The General, Images and Audio tabs keep related controls together.

  • Combine media type, format, collection, tags, import time and auto-tagging status.
  • Match any or all selected tags; the sidebar tag list follows the selected media scope and collection.
  • Filter imports by recent hours, days or a custom number of minutes.
  • Cancel returns to the previous filters and selection. Sections with active filters open automatically.

Search what an image contains.

Use dedicated OCR search to find labels, screenshots and UI references by their recognized text. Use palette colors and coverage to find artwork that belongs together.

  • A contains-text filter distinguishes text-bearing images from artwork without recognized text.
  • Recognized text appears separately from tags and can be copied from the inspector.
  • Pick one or several colors, then choose any-color or all-color matching.
  • Adjust color tolerance and minimum coverage with percentage sliders or exact numeric entry.

Start with something close.

Find Similar uses the selected image or sound as a reference. Results start with the closest 100 matches, with Load More available when you want to keep exploring.

  • Image similarity compares visual appearance using a local index.
  • Audio similarity compares acoustic characteristics such as tone, texture and energy shape.
  • Repeat searches can reuse cached comparisons.
  • Similarity is a ranking, not a guarantee of the same subject or sound meaning.
