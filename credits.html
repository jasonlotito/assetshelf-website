What you’re looking at.

Real app views. Sample media.

The screenshots in this tour are rendered from AssetShelf’s native views using an isolated demonstration library. They are not a web recreation of the app, and they do not contain a personal media library.

The playing-card artwork is from Kenney’s Boardgame Pack, released under CC0. The coin animation and sound examples are synthetic demonstration assets created for this tour. Tags and source notes are sample data.

Some screenshots are close-ups of an inspector or dialog, and others are cropped to show a specific control. Select a screenshot to view its full captured image. Sample counts and filenames describe the demonstration library, not a performance benchmark.

Feature scope

Descriptions are based on the current app and source. Format availability varies with macOS, source files and available encoders. Musical estimates and similarity rankings should be reviewed, not treated as guarantees.

Cloud-folder support relies on the file provider. It is not a Dropbox or iCloud account integration. External AI providers have their own account, usage and privacy terms; local CLI execution is not offline model inference.

Site privacy

This website has no analytics, account system or remote fonts. Search runs in your browser.
