Listen without leaving your library.

Select a sound to preview it in the inspector and use the audio transport while browsing. A waveform gives you a quick view of its envelope before you press play.

  • Play/pause, looping, scrubbing, volume and optional auto-play.
  • Grid previews and list waveforms help distinguish impacts, sustained tones and longer effects.
  • Keep source details, tags and collection memberships beside the player.
  • Preview an original recording or one of its saved audio variants.

Describe sound with useful measurements.

Local signal analysis adds measurements you can inspect and filter without an external AI service. Find a quick attack, a fading tail or a clip in the duration range you need.

  • Duration, attack, decay, envelope shape and fade characteristics.
  • Peak, RMS, crest factor, integrated loudness and true peak.
  • Stereo balance, stereo image, clipping indicators, silence and active duration.
  • Codec, original bit depth, sample rate, channels and reported bitrate.

Tempo and key, with room for judgment.

For music, AssetShelf offers local BPM and musical-key estimates. It can leave uncertain results unavailable rather than assigning every drum hit a key.

  • Tempo and musical-key estimates appear with the other audio properties.
  • A tonic can be recorded without forcing major or minor when the mode is unclear.
  • Correct the values yourself in the inspector or through MCP.
  • Treat estimates as starting points and audition the result; unusual loops and sound effects can be ambiguous.

And the details that make it useful.

Acoustic similarity
Find sounds with nearby tone, texture and energy shape. The ranking does not claim semantic understanding.
Local sound categories
Optional on-device tagging uses Apple Sound Analysis plus filename clues and measured properties.
Many working formats
Browse WAV, MP3, M4A, AIFF, CAF, FLAC and AAC, with bundled Ogg decoding for supported Vorbis/Opus streams.
Export ready for the next app
Create MP3, WAV, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and other supported output formats. Encoders for MP3/Vorbis/Opus ship with AssetShelf.
Keep whole recordings
Conversion processes the recording, not just the preview excerpt. Existing originals remain unchanged.
Understand the analysis
Coverage and analysis details are available in the inspector. Local measurements and optional AI descriptions are separate.
