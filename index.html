A library that works
as hard as your files do.

AssetShelf brings the useful parts of a file browser, a visual reference library and a sound-effects manager together. Explore the workflows below to see exactly what it can do.

Organize

A collection you can actually use.

Bring loose folders, downloaded packs, reference images and sounds into one library. Keep the originals, give everything context, and reuse the same asset across projects.

↗Find

Find the right file, not just its name.

Search words, tags, recognized image text and measured properties. Combine the clues you know, then keep useful searches ready for next time.

↗Images

Get a closer look at every image.

Browse quickly, inspect original pixels, compare alternatives and find the visual details that make an asset useful. Small sprites and large references both get room to breathe.

↗Audio

Find the sound that fits.

Audition reusable effects and music loops, read their waveforms, and filter by what the audio actually does. Keep listening and organizing in the same place.

↗Sprite Sheets

From sprite sheet to usable frames.

Find a grid, refine the boundaries, or import exact atlas metadata. Export the sprites you need and turn selected frames into a reusable animation.

↗Convert

The format you need. The original you keep.

Create another format without starting a second metadata trail. Save variants under the same asset, or export independent copies ready for another app.

↗AI & Agents

Your library, available to your tools.

Keep tagging local, opt into an AI provider when you want it, and give Codex or Claude a searchable media library through MCP. You decide which connections to enable.

↗Library Care

Look after the library you build.

Keep originals, metadata and backups under your control. Check what needs attention, verify a recovery copy, and move the library when your storage needs change.
